How it works

01

Connect your calendars

Give Claw access to the calendars you already use. It reads what's there and builds a clear picture of your schedule without duplicating anything.

02

Claw sends reminders when things are coming up, checks in on tasks that have been sitting too long, and gives you a nudge when you're on a roll.
